Aurore

Aurore seeks to answer the question: How might we use design as a way to help those with trauma? With that said, Aurore aims to aid those experiencing trauma with consistent short-term and long-term support by providing users with the choices of healing rituals, emotional support, mood tracking, and monthly care packages. Everybody has baggage, but not everybody has the support or a way to process it. Aurore is here to ground you in your dawn of healing to become a stronger, healthier version of yourself.
